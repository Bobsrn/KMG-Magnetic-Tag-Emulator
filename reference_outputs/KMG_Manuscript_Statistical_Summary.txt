STATISTICAL METHODS
Each classifier was evaluated in a separate online run; therefore, observations from different classifiers were treated as independent.
ST, CT, and RTA were compared using Kruskal-Wallis tests, followed by Holm-corrected Mann-Whitney U tests. Epsilon-squared and Cliff's delta were used as omnibus and pairwise effect sizes, respectively.
Completion success was compared using Pearson chi-square tests, followed by Holm-corrected two-sided Fisher exact tests. CR values were reported with 95% Wilson confidence intervals.
